# Privacy parameters for code changes

These rules come from the published Privacy Policy (`apps/landingpage/src/content/legal/privacy.md`, served at `/privacy`) and the decisions behind it (Screen Review rows 091, 095, 30 Sep 2026). Every change that collects, stores, sends or displays personal information must follow them. If a change needs a rule to move, update the policy first and bump `CONSENT_POLICY_VERSION`.

Operator: Oneiro N.A., Inc., dba Amplify Digital, Boston, Massachusetts. Contact: privacy@amped.bio.

## Consent and analytics

- Amped's Google Analytics (`G-SK6H61G3S1`) runs on every public page and the editor, in Google Consent Mode.
- Consent defaults to denied for `analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and `ad_personalization`, set before any Google tag runs (`CONSENT_DEFAULT_SNIPPET` in `lib/ampedAnalytics.ts`, and `apps/client/index.html`).
- Only Accept all, or Return visits allowed, grants `analytics_storage`. Amped's own ad consent types always stay denied.
- The editor has no consent card, so it stays cookieless.
- No Google Analytics on `/oauth/*`.
- Withdrawing consent denies storage and deletes `_ga_SK6H61G3S1`, and `_ga` unless a creator's GA4 is active on the page.
- A creator's own GA4, Meta and TikTok tags load only after the visitor allows that creator's tags. They share Amped's gtag queue. When the creator's tags are allowed but Amped analytics is not, set `ga-disable-G-SK6H61G3S1`.
  - This is intended: Consent Mode has one `analytics_storage` state per page, and the creator's GA4 needs it granted, so Amped's property cannot stay cookieless next to it. Amped's property sends nothing for those visitors, not even cookieless pings, until they allow Amped analytics.
  - Withdrawing the creator's tags, or leaving the creator page, calls `revokeCreatorGa`: the creator's GA4 is disabled and, unless Amped analytics is allowed, storage goes back to denied and `_ga` is deleted.
- Page views: `gtag('config')` sends the first one, and GA4 enhanced measurement ("Page changes based on browser history events", on by default) sends one per client side navigation. Do not send `page_view` manually as well, or navigations are counted twice. If that setting is turned off in the property, switch to `send_page_view: false` and send every page view manually.
- Server side events to Meta Conversions API and TikTok Events API are sent only for consented events. Do not store the IP address.
- Global Privacy Control turns off creator tags.
- First party visit counting (`lib/analytics.ts` to `/api/analytics/collect`) is cookieless and stores no IP address. The daily salted visitor hash must not become reversible or cross creator.

## Lifetimes

- 24 months for all visitors, including EU and UK: the consent choice (`CONSENT_MAX_AGE_MS`), the return visit ID (`amped_vid`), and GA cookies (`cookie_expires` 63072000 seconds).
- Retention: visit events 25 months, consent records 5 years (no IP address), accounts deleted within 30 days of closing, wallet and transaction records 5 years after close, ndau conversion records 7 years, connected app records 30 days after removal, support messages 2 years, Telegram link until unlink or account close, Telegram link and delivery choice history 5 years, USDC sends, transfers and Buy USDC purchases 5 years after close (as wallet and transaction records), Coinbase userAuthToken 60 days, screening denies 5 years, messages between fans and creators 12 months after the last message in the conversation, RNS name expiry notices until the account closes, push subscriptions (`push_subscriptions`: endpoint, keys, user agent) until the device unsubscribes, the push service answers 404 or 410, or the account closes, bell rows (`notifications`) and message notification preferences until the account closes, notification jobs (`notification_jobs`) 30 days after they finish (`purgeJobs`), checkout intents (`checkout_intents`: item, creator, source surface, promoter code, status) 30 days after they end unless they became a purchase, identity check results (`identity_checks`: outcome, reason and dates only) until the account closes, under 18 review cases (`identity_review_cases`) 5 years (counsel confirms the period), access events (`access_events`: locked views, checks, refusals and reveals) 25 months.

## Rules for new code

- No new tracker, pixel, SDK or third party recipient without adding it to the policy (sections 8 and 9) first. The X (Twitter) pixel is not in use.
- Nothing is written to cookies or browser storage on public pages before a choice, except what the policy's storage table lists.
- Never send visitor level data to AI providers. Creator summaries send page totals only.
- Minimum account age is 18.
- Authbase attributes (`AUTHBASE_ATTRIBUTE_KEYS`: name on ID, over 18, gender, country) are private to the owner. One reaches a visitor only when the owner is Verified, `RNS_PUBLIC_ATTRIBUTES` is on, and its switch is on in the owner's RNS ID block, applied on the server. Never show the date of birth.
- Access events (Build Board #21, `services/access/engine.ts`) key a signed out viewer by the daily salted visitor hash scoped to the creator (`v:` plus 22 characters), never the IP. Signed in viewers are keyed by account. No refusal or event carries a stake amount.
- Identity checks (Build Board #47, `services/identity/checks.ts`) store only pass or fail, the reason, the tier and the dates, never an attribute value. An under 18 result opens a review case that a person decides (notice section 12); the support alert names the case and handle only.
- Link the policy from new public surfaces and emails: `/privacy` on amped.bio, `new URL("/privacy", env.LANDINGPAGE_URL)` in server emails (so staging links to staging), `https://amped.bio/privacy` elsewhere.
- Copy rules: no income, yield, APY or APR. Use the standard testnet line where tREVO appears.
- Telegram (Build Board #2): store only the Telegram user ID, name, username and photo link from Log In with Telegram; never request the `phone` scope. Encrypt username, name and photo link at rest (`services/telegram/secret.ts`). Never return a Telegram ID to anyone but its owner, never show a creator's Telegram username on a public surface, and show a fan's username to a creator only with the fan's `shareUsername` choice.
- Telegram delivery of creator updates is opt in on the global preference row, recorded in `telegram_link_events` with `CONSENT_POLICY_VERSION`; `/stop` and unlink turn it off everywhere. The bot writes only to a reachable link (`botAccess` true, `blockedAt` null).
- Fan to creator messages (Build Board #2, phase 1): stored as plain text, never scanned. Staff read a conversation only after a report (`admin.telegram.reportedThread` refuses an unreported thread). Never send message text to analytics or AI providers. Purged 12 months after the thread's last message (`purgeStaleMessages`).
- Telegram groups (Build Board #2, phase 2): `telegram_group_members` stores the Telegram user ID (also of people without an Amped.Bio account), the linked user ID if any, and approve, decline and removal times with the reason. Creators and admins see counts only. Rows are deleted when the creator disconnects the group (`disconnectGroup`) or the creator account is deleted (cascade).
- USDC and Buy USDC (Build Board #29 and #5): recipients are Coinbase (CDP relayer and Onramp), Circle (screening) and the Chainalysis sanctions oracle, as policy section 8 lists. Send Coinbase only the wallet address, IP address, amount, region and the opaque `onramp_ref` (`ob_` plus random characters); never the user ID, email, name or phone. Use embedded order mode so Coinbase collects phone and email in its own frame; never store what Coinbase collects. Store the `userAuthToken` encrypted (`services/onramp/secret.ts`) for 60 days and never return it to any browser or export. Screening sends the address only.
- RNS name expiry reminders: the only recipient is SMTP2GO, which gets the account email address and the message, including the RNS name and its expiry date. The `rns_expiry_notices` table stores the user id, the RNS label, the registration expiry, the reminder stage, the time of the reminder and the number of failed sends; the rows go with the account. Reminders go to verified email addresses only, one per name, expiry and stage, and the person can turn them off in Account settings.
- Fan Library (Build Board #26, FL-2): `saved_items` (fan, item, time saved) and `library_creator_visits` (fan, creator, last time the fan opened that creator's section) are private to the fan. Never return them to the creator, an admin list or analytics; the rows go with either account (cascade). Visits are recorded only for creators the fan follows.
- Amped Messages notifications (Build Board #2 r4, PR M4): Web Push recipients are the browser's push service only (Google FCM, Apple, Mozilla, Microsoft WNS), as policy section 8 lists. `push.subscribe` accepts endpoints on those hosts only (`PUSH_SERVICE_HOSTS` in `services/notifications/push.ts`); add a host to the allowlist and the policy together. Store only the endpoint, the `p256dh` and `auth` keys and the user agent. Payloads are encrypted for the device and carry the sender's handle and at most 80 characters of text, never text for a request or an 18+ sender. The service worker (`apps/client/public/sw.js`) is listed in policy section 9; FL-5 adds the cache and fetch handler below.
- Message reminder emails: the only recipient is SMTP2GO, which gets the account email address, the sender's name and handle and the unread count, never the message text. Verified addresses only, at most one per conversation every 6 hours and 3 a day, with a signed one tap off link and the setting in Account, Messages.
- Fan Library offline downloads (Build Board #26, FL-5): a copy lives only in this browser's Cache Storage, under `/downloads/{publicId}`, and the list in `amped.downloads.<account>` (per account, with the last check time), the random `amped.device` id and the `amped.downloads.wifiOnly` choice, as policy section 9 lists. `download_records` stores the fan, the item, the device id, the size, the grant id, the times and a removal reason; the rows go with the account or the item (cascade), and creators, admins and analytics never see them. `amped.device` is a random id made at install, never a fingerprint. One account per device: signing out, or another account signing in, deletes the copies and every other account's list. The service worker caches only the app shell (`index.html`, hashed `/assets/`, icons, the manifest) and `/downloads/{publicId}`; never API responses, signed media URLs, other origins or creator pages. Turning the `fanLibrary` flag off deletes both caches. Offline opens stop 30 days after the last successful check, and a check deletes copies the fan may no longer open, that were taken down, or that the creator no longer allows downloads for.
- Fan Library new items (Build Board #26, FL-6): the bell writes `notifications` rows of kind `CONTENT` with the creator and the item, and `notification_preferences.newContentEnabled` says whether alerts are on, for everyone or for one creator. The daily email's only recipient is SMTP2GO, which gets the account email address, the creator's name and handle, and the titles of items the fan can open. Verified addresses only, at most one email per creator a day, with a signed one tap off link that turns `Follow.email_updates` off for that creator. `notification_jobs` digest rows carry the fan, the creator and the send result, purged 30 days after the send. Never email or alert a title of an item the fan can not open, and never an 18+ title.
- Admin, Money shows handles only, never fan email, names or the `onramp_ref`. Privacy requests for USDC records use `pnpm run --filter server usdc:records <email or handle>`; add `--forget-token` when an account closes.
