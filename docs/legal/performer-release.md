**Purpose:** the release a creator uses with every person who appears in
the creator's 18+ content. It is the creator's document. Amped supplies
it in the app and stores the signed copy. Amped is not a party to it.

# Part A. How it works in the app

1.  The creator tags each person who appears when submitting an 18+
    item. A solo creator who is the only person in the item needs no
    release.

2.  Each tagged person receives a link. The person passes the Authbase
    Over 18 check, reads the release, sets the options, and signs.

3.  The person gives the creator a copy of a valid government picture
    ID, legal name, date of birth and every other name used, for the
    creator's records under 18 U.S.C. 2257. This happens between the two
    of them. It does not pass through Amped.

4.  The creator countersigns. The item can now go to review.

5.  Amped stores the signed release, both signature times, the Authbase
    result and reference, and the item reference. Amped does not store
    ID copies.

6.  If the person withdraws consent, the item is hidden on receipt and
    comes off sale.

- Fans lose access to the Content.

Withdrawal does not affect anything lawfully done before it.
