## 1. Analytics we provide

1.1 Amped Bio provides you with statistics about visits to your page, such as views, visitors,
link clicks, traffic sources, locations, devices, returning visitors and members who joined through
your page.

1.2 Statistics are provided in aggregate. You will not receive the identity, IP address or
account of individual visitors. Data exports contain event records without visitor or account
identifiers.

1.3 Returning visitor and retention statistics include only visitors who allowed return-visit
measurement. They are estimates and are labelled with their coverage.

1.4 Analytics are provided free of charge [for now]. Amped Bio may change the features, history
or limits of analytics with [30 days'] notice.

## 2. Tracking tools you connect

2.1 You may connect your own Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Meta Conversions API, TikTok Pixel or
TikTok Events API ("Your Tools") to your page.

2.2 Your Tools load only for visitors who give consent through the Amped Bio banner. You must not
attempt to load tracking technology on your page by any other means, including through embedded
content or link text.

2.3 For data collected through Your Tools, you are [the controller / a joint controller with
Amped Bio for the collection only]. You are responsible for:
  (a) having a lawful basis and complying with applicable privacy law, including GDPR, UK GDPR,
      ePrivacy rules and US state privacy laws, for your use of that data;
  (b) complying with the terms and policies of Google, Meta and TikTok;
  (c) not using Your Tools to collect sensitive data or data about children;
  (d) honouring requests from visitors about data held in Your Tools.

2.4 When you enter an access token for a server-side tool, you authorise Amped Bio to send
consented page view and link click events, including the visitor's IP address and browser
details, to that provider on your behalf. Amped Bio acts as your [processor] for that transfer
under the [Data Processing Addendum].

2.5 Amped Bio stores your access tokens encrypted and uses them only to send events you
authorised. You can remove a token at any time from Analytics settings. Amped Bio may disable a
tool that it reasonably believes is misused or that a provider reports.

## 3. Campaign links

3.1 Campaign links and QR codes you create carry a campaign identifier. Statistics for a campaign
cover visits that arrive through that link only.

## 4. No warranty on figures

4.1 Statistics are provided for information. They may differ from figures in other tools because
of ad blockers, consent choices, bot filtering and differences in definitions. [Standard
limitation of liability applies.]
