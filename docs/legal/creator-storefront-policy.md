**Purpose:** a store policy each creator can adopt as their own. The
creator is the seller, so the store policy speaks in the creator's name.
Amped supplies a standard version that fills in the creator's name, or
the creator can publish their own.

# Part A. How it works in the product

## A.1 Two options in Store settings

| **Option**                        | **What happens**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Use the standard policy (default) | Amped fills the template in Part B with the creator's details. The creator chooses the seller name: display name, or a legal or business name. The creator picks a refund option and a contact method, previews, and publishes. The 18+ section appears only when 18+ is on. |
| Use my own policy                 | The creator pastes or uploads their own text. Before it publishes, the creator confirms each required statement in A.3 is covered. Amped does not write or approve the creator's text.                                                                                       |

## A.2 Shown on every store, whichever option

A fixed notice sits above every store policy. The creator cannot edit or
remove it:

> Sold by {{seller_name}} on Amped.bio. Your purchase is from
> {{seller_name}}, not from Amped. The Amped.bio Terms, the Contract
> between Fan and Creator and the Adult Content Policy also apply, and
> they prevail if anything in this store policy conflicts with them.

## A.3 Required statements for a custom policy

The creator confirms the custom policy:

- Names the creator as the seller.

- States buyers must be 18 or older, and that 18+ content requires the
  Amped age check.

- States what the buyer gets: a personal, limited licence to view, not
  ownership.

- States the refund terms, in USDC to the Amped wallet.

- For memberships, states renewal, the 3 day reminder, the 7 day grace
  period and how to cancel.

- If 18+ is on, states that everyone who appears was 18 or older, passed
  the Amped check and signed a release, and that the creator keeps 2257
  records.

A custom policy may not:

- Contradict the Amped Terms, the Contract between Fan and Creator or
  the Adult Content Policy.

- Take payment, or direct buyers to pay, outside Amped checkout.

- Offer in-person meetings or services.

- Promise refunds or payment methods Amped does not support.

A custom policy that breaks these rules can be removed under the Adult
Content Policy, and the standard policy shows in its place.

# Part B. Standard policy text

**{{store_name}} Store Policy**

Effective {{effective_date}}

## 1. Who you are buying from

This store is run by {{seller_name}} (@{{handle}}). When you buy
content or a membership here, you buy it from me. I am the seller.
Amped.bio provides the storefront and checkout. Amped is not the seller.

## 2. Age

You must be 18 or older to buy from this store. To see any 18+ content,
you must pass the Amped age check. Do not share your account or let
anyone under 18 use it.

## 3. What you get

When you buy an item or join a membership, you get a personal, limited
licence to view that content on your own devices through Amped. You do
not own the content. You may not download it outside Amped's features,
record it, share it, repost it, resell it or use it to train AI.

One time purchases stay in your Library while the item is in my store
and your account is in good standing. Membership content is available
while your membership is active.

## 4. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in USDC, for example \$10 USDC. You pay with USDC from
your Amped wallet through Amped checkout. The price you see is the price
you pay. Any network fee is shown before you confirm. Payments run on a
public blockchain, so the wallet address used is visible on chain.

## 5. Memberships

- Memberships are monthly or yearly at the price shown when you join.

- Your membership renews each period. You get a reminder 3 days before
  each charge.

- If your wallet balance is short at renewal, you have a 7 day grace
  period to top up. After that, your membership ends.

- You can cancel at any time in My Memberships. You keep access to the
  end of the period you paid for.

- If I change a membership price, the new price applies from your next
  period after I give notice.

## 6. Refunds

{{refund_terms}}

**Option A (default):** Digital content opens as soon as your payment
confirms, so sales are final. If an item does not open or is not as
described, message me within 7 days and I will fix it or refund you in
full.

**Option B:** You may ask for a refund within {{refund_days}} days
of purchase for any reason. Message me and I will refund you in full.

Refunds are paid in USDC to your Amped wallet. The law where you live
may give you more rights. Nothing in this policy limits them.

## 7. Custom requests

If I accept a custom request, we agree the description, price and
delivery time in messages before you pay. I only make content within my
own rules and the Amped Adult Content Policy. I may decline any request.

## 8. Messages and conduct

Be respectful. No harassment, threats, requests to meet in person, or
requests for anything illegal. I may block anyone who breaks these
rules.

## 9. My content (shown when 18+ is on)

Everyone who appears in my content was 18 or older when it was made,
passed the Amped age check and signed a release. I keep the records
required by 18 U.S.C. 2257. I follow the Amped.bio Adult Content Policy.

## 10. Leaks and copyright

My content is mine. Sharing it outside Amped breaks this policy and may
break the law. If you do, I may end your access and send takedown
notices.

## 11. Privacy

I can see your Amped handle, what you buy from me and the messages you
send me. I cannot see your legal name, your age check details or your
wallet balance. Amped's privacy notice explains how Amped handles your
information.

## 12. Problems and reports

Contact me through {{contact_method}}. If you appear in content on
Amped without your consent, use the Take It Down form on Amped.bio.
Amped acts within 48 hours. To report anything else that breaks the
rules, use the report link on the item.

## 13. Changes

I may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest
version. The version in force when you buy applies to that purchase.
