Use the name and mark exactly as written so users recognise the account they are signing in with.

## Button copy

- **Do** use "Sign in with Amped.bio" or "Continue with Amped.bio".
- **Do not** use "Login with Amped", "Amped login", "amped.bio OAuth" or translated variants.

## Button markup

```html
<button type="button" class="amped-sign-in">
  <svg width="18" height="18" viewBox="0 0 24 24" aria-hidden="true">
    <!-- Amped.bio mark -->
  </svg>
  Sign in with Amped.bio
</button>
```

Keep the mark at least 18px tall, preserve its proportions, and never place it on a low contrast background.

## Colours

| Usage | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Primary button background | `#2563EB` |
| Button label | `#FFFFFF` |
| Outline button border | `#D1D5DB` |
| Outline button label | `#111827` |

## Consent screen

Our consent screen is hosted by us and cannot be restyled. You may show your own explanation of what you do
with the data before redirecting, but you must not ask users to hand over their Amped.bio password anywhere
except on our own pages.

## Support

Questions about an integration, rate limits or a redirect URI change? Contact the Amped.bio team through the
support channel listed in your application's review email.
