# Amped.Bio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Amped.Bio collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to creators, fans and anyone who visits an Amped.Bio page.

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Oneiro N.A., Inc., doing business as Amplify Digital, Boston, Massachusetts ("Amplify Digital", "Amped.Bio", "we") runs amped.bio. We decide how the personal information described here is used, except where a section says the creator decides.

This policy covers:

- The Amped.Bio editor, account and wallet, including USDC on Base and Buy USDC through Coinbase
- Public creator pages at amped.bio/handle and the public site (home page, pools, blog and developer docs)
- Sign in with Amped.Bio, when you use your account to sign in to another app
- The Amped.Bio bot on Telegram, when you link your Telegram account
- Emails we send you, and notifications from Amped.Bio on your devices

It does not cover the creator's own tools on their page, or apps you connect through Sign in with Amped.Bio. Those follow their own privacy policies.

Contact: privacy@amped.bio. We have not appointed a data protection officer or an EU or UK representative, as neither is required for us today.

## 2. Information you give us

When you create an account and build your page, we collect:

- **Account:** name, email address, password (stored only as a one way hash), and whether your email is verified.
- **Google sign in:** if you sign in with Google, the identifiers and tokens Google returns so we can keep you signed in.
- **Security:** two factor settings and backup codes (stored protected), and a record of each signed in session with its IP address and browser details.
- **Your page:** handle, bio, photo, blocks, links, theme and anything else you publish. Your page is public by design.
- **Uploads:** images and files you upload, stored with our storage provider.
- **Promoter codes:** if you give someone a code that pays them a share of your sales, or if a creator gives you one, we record the code, both accounts, the share and the code's sales and earnings. A promoter sees how many sales their code brought and what they earned, never who bought.
- **X import:** if you import from X, the profile details you choose to bring in.
- **Browser check:** a proof of work check on sign up and sign in to stop automated abuse. It runs on our own service and sets no cookies.
- **Messages to us:** what you send to support.
- **Your Library:** when you are signed in, the items you save to your Library and when you saved them, and when you last opened each followed creator's section of your Library, so we can show what is new. Only you see them. Creators do not see what you saved or when you visited.
- **Message notifications:** when you get a message on Amped.Bio, we add it to the bell in the app. If you allow notifications on a device, your browser gives us a push address for that device (a web address at your browser's push service and two keys that encrypt what we send) and the browser details it reports. We store them to notify you on that device. If a message is still unread after 30 minutes, we may email you a reminder that names the sender but never includes the message text. We store whether notifications and reminder emails are on, and which reminders we sent.
- **New items from creators:** when a creator you follow, or whose pool you stake in, posts an item you can open, we may add it to the bell in the app. If you chose Email me updates when you followed a creator, we may send you at most one email a day listing that creator's new items you can open. We store whether these alerts are on, for every creator or for one, and which emails we sent.

If you verify your identity with Authbase, we receive your verification status and badge, and only the attributes you choose to share with Amped.Bio: your name as checked on your ID, whether you are over 18, your gender and your country. We never receive your date of birth. Only you can see those shared attributes unless you choose to show one. You can show each attribute on your own, while you are verified, by turning it on in your page's RNS ID block. Anyone who views your page or your RNS name can then see it. Others see only your verification status, and only where you choose to show it, plus the attributes you turned on.

When we check your age or your seller status with Authbase, we keep the result of the check and when it ends. We never receive your birth date or ID.

**Buying a check a business runs.** Some identity checks are sold by a business seller, for example Authbase, and that business runs the check after you pay. We give that seller your order so it can do the work: an order reference we make, the product you bought, the amount, the wallet address that paid, the RNS name you entered, and the order and check status. We do not give it your email, your legal name, your wallet balance or any ID document. The seller runs the check under its own privacy policy and never sends us your ID document or selfie.

**Business sellers and their teams.** If a business applies to sell on Amped.Bio, or an account is added to a business seller's team, we collect the business name, its registration details, information about its beneficial owners, the result of the sanctions screen on the business, its owners and its payout wallet, the payout wallet address and the signed proof that the business controls it, and the accounts, names, emails and roles on the team. We also keep an audit log of each change and the names, prefixes and last use of the API keys and the webhook addresses the business creates. We use these to approve the business, pay it, keep its account secure and meet our legal and tax duties.

## 3. Wallets, tREVO and on chain activity

Your Amped.Bio wallet is created through Web3Auth, which receives the sign in details it needs to secure your wallet key. We store your wallet address with your account.

When you send tREVO, stake in a pool, claim rewards, create a pool, register or transfer a name, or refer someone, we record the transaction details with your account: amounts, addresses, pool, transaction ID and time. Referrals record who referred whom.

**Blockchain records are public and permanent.** Transactions on the Revolution Network, including your wallet address and amounts, can be seen by anyone and cannot be changed or deleted by us. Pool pages and unlisted pool detail pages show public contract data for a pool, including its creator and totals.

**ndau conversion.** If you convert ndau, we store your ndau address, REVO address, amounts, the signatures and validation key you provide, the document hash of the terms you accepted, the status and any transaction ID. We keep these as the record of your request.

Testnet only. Pool rewards come from the network, vary, and are not guaranteed.

**USDC on Base.** Your wallet can also hold USDC on Base, using the same wallet address. When you send USDC, you sign the transfer in your wallet and Coinbase's server wallet submits it for us and pays the network fee. We record each send with your account: amount, sending and receiving addresses, status, transaction ID and time. We also record USDC that reaches or leaves your address, and who on Amped.Bio sent or received it. Transactions on Base, including your wallet address and amounts, are public and permanent.

**Address screening.** Before a send, and when you first use USDC, we check the receiving address and your own address against sanctions and risk lists, using Circle and the Chainalysis sanctions oracle. We send only the wallet address. If an address is refused, we keep the address, the vendor's reference and the time.

**Buy USDC through Coinbase.** Coinbase sells you the USDC and delivers it to your wallet. Amped.Bio never receives your card or bank details and charges nothing. When you buy, we send Coinbase your wallet address, your IP address, the amount, your region, and a random reference we made for you instead of your account ID or email. If you pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay, Coinbase asks for your phone number and email and verifies you inside its own window on our page, under Coinbase's privacy policy. We do not receive those details. Coinbase gives us a sign in token so you can skip that step next time; we store it encrypted for 60 days. We record each purchase: way to pay, amount, Coinbase's price and fees, status, Coinbase's order number and the transaction ID. Coinbase's own [privacy policy](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/privacy) covers what Coinbase collects.

## 4. Visitors to creator pages and the public site

We measure visits so creators can see how their page performs and so we can run and improve Amped.Bio. A consent card on each creator page lets you choose Accept all, Reject all or Choose. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy choices at the bottom of the page.

### Amped.Bio visit counting (always, no cookies)

On every visit we record the page, the links you tap, how long the page was visible, the site or campaign that sent you, your approximate country and city, and your device type, operating system and browser. We do not store your IP address. To count distinct visitors we make a one way code from your network address, browser details, the creator's page and a secret that changes every day. It cannot be reversed or used to recognise you on another day or another creator's page. Known bots are dropped. Creators viewing their own page while signed in are not counted.

### Google Analytics (always, cookies only with your permission)

We use Google Analytics, run by Google, on creator pages, the public site and the editor. Until you choose, it runs in Consent Mode: it sends page views, device information and your approximate location, which Google works out from your IP address, and it sets no cookies. If you choose Accept all or allow Return visits on a creator page, Google Analytics also sets cookies to recognise return visits for up to 24 months. In the editor it always runs without cookies. We do not use Google Analytics on Sign in with Amped.Bio pages.

### Return visits (only with your permission)

If you allow Return visits, we also save a random Amped.Bio ID in your browser so creators can see returning visitors. It is coded per creator, so creators cannot link your visits across different creators' pages. If you are signed in to Amped.Bio, we record your account with the visit. Creators see only totals, never which account visited. Withdrawing your permission deletes the ID and the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal of creator tags.

### Locked items

When you open or try to open a locked item, we record that it happened so the creator can see how their locks perform. If you are signed in, we record your account; otherwise we use the same daily one way code as visit counting. Creators see totals and the handles of fans who unlocked, never your stake.

### Summaries for creators

A creator can ask for a written summary of their page statistics. To write it, we send Anthropic, our AI provider, that page's totals for the period and the period before, top sources, devices, visitor counts and up to 10 link names. No visitor level data is sent.

## 5. Creator tags on creator pages (only with your permission)

Some creators connect their own Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) or TikTok to their page. The consent card names the creator and each service they use. These tags load only if you choose Accept all or allow Ads and analytics by that creator. They set their own cookies and receive your IP address and device information, and use it under their own privacy policies: [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy).

If the creator also connected Meta Conversions API or TikTok Events API, we send the same page view or link click from our servers, including your IP address and browser details, only if you allowed that creator's tags. We do not store your IP address for this.

Your choice is saved per creator. The creator decides which tools to connect and how to use the data, so the creator is responsible for it. For the server side events, Amplify Digital sends the data on the creator's instructions and does not use it for its own purposes.

## 6. Sign in with Amped.Bio

When you sign in to another app with Amped.Bio, the consent screen names the app and lists what it will receive. We share only what you allow, for example your name and picture, your wallet address, or your email address. We keep a record of which apps you allowed, what they can access and when.

You can remove an app at any time in Account settings, under Connected apps. Removing it ends its access at once. Access tokens it already holds stop working within 15 minutes. What the app did with your information before that follows the app's own privacy policy.

Some Amped.Bio apps may skip the consent screen. They still appear in Connected apps.

If you register an app as a developer, we store the app's name, website, redirect addresses and credentials.

### Telegram

You can link your Telegram account in Account settings, under Connected apps. Linking uses Telegram's own sign in. We receive your Telegram user ID, your name, your username if you have one, and a link to your profile photo. We never receive your phone number, your contacts or your other chats. We store your username, name and photo link encrypted.

We use the link to deliver updates from creators you follow to your Telegram, but only after you turn Get creator updates on Telegram on. Each time you turn it on or off we record the choice, when you made it and the policy version, with no IP address. Sending /stop to the bot turns it off for every creator.

A creator never sees your Telegram username unless you turn on Let creators see my Telegram username. A creator who shows the Telegram mark on their page never shows their username either.

When you message a creator through Amped.Bio, or a creator replies to you, we store the messages and show them in both inboxes on Amped.Bio. When the other side linked Telegram, the Amped.Bio bot also delivers a copy there. We do not read conversations unless one side reports one; then our staff read that conversation to review the report. We keep messages for 12 months after the last message in a conversation, then delete them.

When a creator connects a Telegram group to Amped.Bio, the Amped.Bio bot checks who may join it. For each person who asks to join, joins or leaves that group, we store their Telegram user ID, whether it matches a linked Amped.Bio account, and when they were approved, declined or removed, with the reason. We store this even for people who never linked Telegram to Amped.Bio, because the bot needs it to apply the creator's rule. The creator sees counts only, never who. We delete these records when the creator disconnects the group or closes their account.

Unlink at any time under Connected apps. Unlinking stops all Telegram delivery at once and deletes the link. Your messages stay in your Amped.Bio inbox. Telegram's own privacy policy covers how Telegram handles your messages.

## 11. Your choices and rights

**Choices you can make now:**

- Change your privacy choices on any creator page from Privacy choices. Reject all is as easy as Accept all.
- Edit or remove your page content and uploads in the editor.
- Remove connected apps in Account settings, under Connected apps.
- Turn off name expiry reminders in Account settings, under Name reminders.
- Turn off message notifications or message reminder emails in Account settings, under Messages, or turn off reminder emails from the link in any reminder email. You can also block notifications for Amped.Bio in your browser or device settings.
- Turn off alerts for new items in Account settings, under Messages, or for one creator in their Library section. Turn off a creator's daily email in their Library section or from the link in the email.
- Turn on two factor sign in.
- Close your account by writing to privacy@amped.bio from the email on your account.

**Your rights.** Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your information, object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before. In some US states you can also opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as that opt out. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

To use a right, write to privacy@amped.bio. We may need to confirm it is you. We answer within one month, or sooner where your local law sets a shorter time. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

We cannot change or delete records on the blockchain, including your wallet address and transactions.

## 12. Transfers, security, children and changes

**International transfers.** Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and Germany. Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on the EU US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where a provider is certified, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK addendum.

**Security.** Passwords are stored as one way hashes. Creator Meta and TikTok tokens and Coinbase sign in tokens are encrypted at rest and never sent to any browser. Access to personal information is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure.

**Children.** You must be 18 or older to create an Amped.Bio account, because accounts include a wallet and tokens. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, write to us and we will delete it. If an age check shows that someone on an account is under 18, a person on our team reviews it and we close the account.

**Changes.** We will post changes here and update the date at the top. For material changes to how visitors are measured, the consent card asks every visitor again.
