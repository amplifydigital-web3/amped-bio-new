# Amped.Bio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Amped.Bio collects, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to creators, fans and anyone who visits an Amped.Bio page.

## 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Oneiro N.A., Inc., doing business as Amplify Digital, Boston, Massachusetts ("Amplify Digital", "Amped.Bio", "we") runs amped.bio. We decide how the personal information described here is used, except where a section says the creator decides.

This policy covers:

- The Amped.Bio editor, account and wallet
- Public creator pages at amped.bio/handle and the public site (home page, pools, blog and developer docs)
- Sign in with Amped.Bio, when you use your account to sign in to another app
- Emails we send you

It does not cover the creator's own tools on their page, or apps you connect through Sign in with Amped.Bio. Those follow their own privacy policies.

Contact: privacy@amped.bio. We have not appointed a data protection officer or an EU or UK representative, as neither is required for us today.

## 2. Information you give us

When you create an account and build your page, we collect:

- **Account:** name, email address, password (stored only as a one way hash), and whether your email is verified.
- **Google sign in:** if you sign in with Google, the identifiers and tokens Google returns so we can keep you signed in.
- **Security:** two factor settings and backup codes (stored protected), and a record of each signed in session with its IP address and browser details.
- **Your page:** handle, bio, photo, blocks, links, theme and anything else you publish. Your page is public by design.
- **Uploads:** images and files you upload, stored with our storage provider.
- **X import:** if you import from X, the profile details you choose to bring in.
- **Browser check:** a proof of work check on sign up and sign in to stop automated abuse. It runs on our own service and sets no cookies.
- **Messages to us:** what you send to support.

If you verify your identity with Authbase, we receive your verification status and badge, and only the attributes you choose to share with Amped.Bio. Anyone who views your name page can see those shared attributes.

## 3. Wallets, tREVO and on chain activity

Your Amped.Bio wallet is created through Web3Auth, which receives the sign in details it needs to secure your wallet key. We store your wallet address with your account.

When you send tREVO, stake in a pool, claim rewards, create a pool, register or transfer a name, or refer someone, we record the transaction details with your account: amounts, addresses, pool, transaction ID and time. Referrals record who referred whom.

**Blockchain records are public and permanent.** Transactions on the Revolution Network, including your wallet address and amounts, can be seen by anyone and cannot be changed or deleted by us. Pool pages and unlisted pool detail pages show public contract data for a pool, including its creator and totals.

**ndau conversion.** If you convert ndau, we store your ndau address, REVO address, amounts, the signatures and validation key you provide, the document hash of the terms you accepted, the status and any transaction ID. We keep these as the record of your request.

Testnet only. tREVO has no cash value. Pool rewards come from the network, vary, and are not guaranteed.

## 4. Visitors to creator pages and the public site

We measure visits so creators can see how their page performs and so we can run and improve Amped.Bio. A consent card on each creator page lets you choose Accept all, Reject all or Choose. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy choices at the bottom of the page.

### Amped.Bio visit counting (always, no cookies)

On every visit we record the page, the links you tap, how long the page was visible, the site or campaign that sent you, your approximate country and city, and your device type, operating system and browser. We do not store your IP address. To count distinct visitors we make a one way code from your network address, browser details, the creator's page and a secret that changes every day. It cannot be reversed or used to recognise you on another day or another creator's page. Known bots are dropped. Creators viewing their own page while signed in are not counted.

### Google Analytics (always, cookies only with your permission)

We use Google Analytics, run by Google, on creator pages, the public site and the editor. Until you choose, it runs in Consent Mode: it sends page views, device information and your approximate location, which Google works out from your IP address, and it sets no cookies. If you choose Accept all or allow Return visits on a creator page, Google Analytics also sets cookies to recognise return visits for up to 24 months. In the editor it always runs without cookies. We do not use Google Analytics on Sign in with Amped.Bio pages.

### Return visits (only with your permission)

If you allow Return visits, we also save a random Amped.Bio ID in your browser so creators can see returning visitors. It is coded per creator, so creators cannot link your visits across different creators' pages. If you are signed in to Amped.Bio, we record your account with the visit. Creators see only totals, never which account visited. Withdrawing your permission deletes the ID and the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal of creator tags.

### Summaries for creators

A creator can ask for a written summary of their page statistics. We send only totals for that page to Anthropic, our AI provider, to write it. No visitor level data is sent.

## 5. Creator tags on creator pages (only with your permission)

Some creators connect their own Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) or TikTok to their page. The consent card names the creator and each service they use. These tags load only if you choose Accept all or allow Ads and analytics by that creator. They set their own cookies and receive your IP address and device information, and use it under their own privacy policies: [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy).

If the creator also connected Meta Conversions API or TikTok Events API, we send the same page view or link click from our servers, including your IP address and browser details, only if you allowed that creator's tags. We do not store your IP address for this.

Your choice is saved per creator. The creator decides which tools to connect and how to use the data, so the creator is responsible for it. For the server side events, Amplify Digital sends the data on the creator's instructions and does not use it for its own purposes.

## 6. Sign in with Amped.Bio

When you sign in to another app with Amped.Bio, the consent screen names the app and lists what it will receive. We share only what you allow, for example your name and picture, your wallet address, or your email address. We keep a record of which apps you allowed, what they can access and when.

You can remove an app at any time in Account settings, under Connected apps. Removing it ends its access at once. Access tokens it already holds stop working within 15 minutes. What the app did with your information before that follows the app's own privacy policy.

Some Amped.Bio apps may skip the consent screen. They still appear in Connected apps.

If you register an app as a developer, we store the app's name, website, redirect addresses and credentials.

## 11. Your choices and rights

**Choices you can make now:**

- Change your privacy choices on any creator page from Privacy choices. Reject all is as easy as Accept all.
- Edit or remove your page content and uploads in the editor.
- Remove connected apps in Account settings, under Connected apps.
- Turn on two factor sign in.
- Close your account by writing to privacy@amped.bio from the email on your account.

**Your rights.** Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your information, object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before. In some US states you can also opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as that opt out. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

To use a right, write to privacy@amped.bio. We may need to confirm it is you. We answer within one month, or sooner where your local law sets a shorter time. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

We cannot change or delete records on the blockchain, including your wallet address and transactions.

## 12. Transfers, security, children and changes

**International transfers.** Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States and Germany. Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on the EU US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension where a provider is certified, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK addendum.

**Security.** Passwords are stored as one way hashes. Creator Meta and TikTok tokens are encrypted at rest and never sent to any browser. Access to personal information is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure.

**Children.** You must be 18 or older to create an Amped.Bio account, because accounts include a wallet and tokens. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

**Changes.** We will post changes here and update the date at the top. For material changes to how visitors are measured, the consent card asks every visitor again.
